Hearth Attack

In October 2023, as part of a team bonding session, the WaxHeart Team held a 48-Hour Game Jam under the theme "Heart Attack". We took the theme literally and came up with the concept of the player causing a heart attack. I served as Gameplay Programmer on the team, writing the movement, collision, audio hooking, and damage functions, including the screen shaking. This is the first time I ever worked on a game jam that had such a little amount of time and I quite enjoyed the experience. I can very much see myself attending and developing at more in the future. Developed in Unity, written in C#

Release Date: October 23rd, 2023.

Platform: PC
